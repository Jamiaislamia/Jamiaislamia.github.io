Photos folder - drop your images into the sub-folders and they appear on the website (explore.html).

Albums:
  Campus      - campus, buildings, grounds
  Students    - students, classes, study time
  Classrooms  - classrooms, teaching, blackboards
  Mosque      - mosque, prayer, tilawat
  Events      - programmes, competitions, ceremonies
  Programmes  - hifz/nurani/nazera programme activities

How to add photos:
  1. Copy the image files into the right album folder (JPG/PNG/WEBP).
  2. Run: node _build_photos.js   (from the project root)
  3. Refresh the site - the new photos are listed automatically.

On GitHub Pages the Explore page can also read the folders live (no rebuild needed).
Filenames become captions: 'campus-main-gate.jpg' -> 'Campus Main Gate'.
